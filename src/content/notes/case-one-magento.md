**Project:** Lilly Drogerie, at Bee IT.

## The problem

The web shop and the marketing website were separate systems. Content and products drifted apart, and every campaign had to be built twice.

## The approach

Bring both onto one Magento 2 platform: marketing pages and product pages served by the same system, sharing the same catalogue, navigation and design.

## Along the way

- A loyalty programme built into the shop.
- UX improvements across browsing and checkout.
- Full stack work across PHP and JavaScript.

## What I took from it

Consolidating platforms is mostly about content migration and agreeing who owns what. The code is the smaller half.
