At Florbs I started on React and TypeScript, then worked on the Python APIs behind them, and later on Go and Kotlin services. Learning a language on a live product is different from learning it from a book. What helps me:

- **Read the codebase before the tutorial.** The team's conventions matter more than the language's full feature list.
- **Learn the toolchain first.** How to run, test, debug and format. Once that loop is fast, everything else is quicker to learn.
- **Start with small changes.** A small endpoint, a migration, a test, reviewed by people who know the language well.
- **Map, don't translate.** Go's errors and Kotlin's null safety aren't TypeScript with different syntax. Write them the way the language wants.
- **Keep notes.** Things that surprised me go in a list; that list is how this shelf started.
